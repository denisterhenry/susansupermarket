Susan Supermarket is a neighbourhood shop for food and household essentials. The team serves customers in South Sudan. The owner can follow the business from Morocco.

Plan a visit See what we sell

The shop

A supermarket people can count on.

Fresh and packaged food

Rice, bread, oil, sugar, milk, water, and the daily items families come back for.

Clear prices

Goods are sold in South Sudanese pounds, with a record of what came in and what went out.

A staffed counter

Cashiers take sales. A manager watches stock, suppliers, and the cash drawer.

From afar

The owner does not have to be in the aisle.

Sales and stock

Each sale and delivery is written down, so the shelf and the books tell the same story.

Cash and costs

Expenses, supplier payments, and the drawer are kept apart from the selling price.

People and roles

The owner, the manager, and the cashier each see only the work that belongs to them.

A private desk

The working accounts stay private. This public page is for friends and customers to read, the same way a shop sign is public.
